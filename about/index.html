About Reformation Resources

A living, confessionally faithful library for CRC congregations, where the material is a starting place rather than a finished product.

Starting places, not filing cabinets

Most resource libraries are a cabinet of PDFs, and nothing living comes out of them. Here, every lesson, sermon, and liturgy is a starting point that expects to be changed. When a church adapts something for its own people, that version can come back into the library, carrying its congregation’s soil with it.

Make it your own

The main button on every resource is Make it your own. A few plain questions ask who will be in the room and what needs to land differently, and then the text is yours to change. That moment belongs to the pastor or leader alone; no machine writes it for you. Using something as-is is always welcome too. What flows back into the library is your own work, or work you have made your own.

AI serves, the shepherd leads

Behind the scenes, software does the tireless background work: converting files, organizing the library, and helping you find related material. The pastoral acts of writing, adapting, and discerning what your people need stay in human hands.

Read against the Three Forms of Unity

Before a resource joins the library, a first reader checks it against the Belgic Confession, the Heidelberg Catechism, and the Canons of Dort. It is a gracious editor, not a bouncer: anything it notices goes privately to the author, never onto the resource.

Credit given, not grabbed

Every resource keeps a quiet version history. The church that wrote the first version is remembered truthfully, even as others adapt it.
