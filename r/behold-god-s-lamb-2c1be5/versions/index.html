How churches made it their own

2 churches have adapted Bethel CRC’s Behold God's Lamb for their own people. Here is what each one changed, in their words.

  • Cedar Lane CRC, Pella, Iowademo · Sep 27, 2026

    Behold God's Lamb (for a farm community at harvest)

    For whole congregation
    I swapped the opening list of worries for the ones our people are actually carrying at harvest, and I rewrote the call to witness so it sends people to the co-op and the bleachers, not just 'the world.' The heart of the sermon, and the Carson quote, I left alone.
    2 churches served · Rated 5.0 of 5 by 1 church
  • Stony Creek CRC, Grand Rapids, Michigandemo · Oct 4, 2026

    Behold God's Lamb (youth group, with a card activity)

    For middle and high school youth group
    I turned the opening question into an anonymous card activity so students actually answer it, rewrote the list of worries to fit our kids, and added small-group questions at the end so they talk about it instead of just hearing it.
    New to the library · Not yet rated
