How churches made it their own

1 church has adapted Cedar Lane CRC’s Behold God's Lamb (for a farm community at harvest) for their own people. Here is what each one changed, in their words.

  • Prairie Light CRC, Sioux Center, Iowademo · Oct 10, 2026

    Behold God's Lamb (Lenten midweek service)

    For adult, midweek Lent service
    I started from Cedar Lane's version because their farm-season opening fits Sioux County. I cut the Matthew detour to keep the message under fifteen minutes and wrapped it in a call to worship, a prayer of confession with the Agnus Dei, and an assurance of pardon.
