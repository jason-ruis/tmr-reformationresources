Share a resource

A lesson, sermon, study, or liturgy your church has used, or a version of something here that you made your own.

How it will work

  1. Send it the way you already have it. A Word document, a Google Doc, a PDF, or plain text. No new app to learn.
  2. A first reader looks it over. Before anything joins the library, it is read against the Three Forms of Unity: the Belgic Confession, the Heidelberg Catechism, and the Canons of Dort.
  3. Anything it notices comes only to you. Notes go privately to the author, with the passage, the confessional reference, and a suggested sharpening. There are no public flags or markers of any kind.
  4. It joins the library, credited to your church, ready for other congregations to borrow and make their own.

During the prototype

Resources are being added by invitation while the library takes shape. If your church would like to take part, reach out through The Messy Reformation.
